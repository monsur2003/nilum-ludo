Three ways to play

1 vs 1

Red against Yellow, head to head.

2 vs 2 Teams

Red & Yellow against Green & Blue. Finish first and you move your partner's tokens.

4 Players

Everyone for themselves.

Playing with friends

Tap Play Online → Create Room. You get a code like NL4K9P. Send it on WhatsApp or Messenger. Friends tap Join Room, type the code, and show up in your lobby.
